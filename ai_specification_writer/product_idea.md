# Product Idea: StudyFlow AI

## Vision
Help university students manage demanding coursework through one practical workspace that turns course deadlines, study materials, and available time into realistic, adaptable study plans. StudyFlow AI aims to reduce missed deadlines and make studying more consistent without replacing students' own learning or decision-making.

## Problem
Students often track assignments in several places, such as learning platforms, calendars, chats, and personal notes. This makes it difficult to see what is due, prioritize competing tasks, and adjust plans when a class or assignment takes longer than expected. Generic to-do apps can record tasks but rarely account for course difficulty, upcoming exams, or actual study availability.

## Primary Users and Needs
- **University students:** Need a single view of assignments, exams, and priorities, with achievable study sessions based on their weekly schedules.
- **Working students:** Need plans that fit around part-time jobs, commuting, and changing availability.
- **Study-group members:** Need a simple way to coordinate shared milestones while keeping private study tasks separate.

## Key Features
1. **Course and deadline dashboard:** Create courses, add tasks and exams, set due dates, and see upcoming work in one place.
2. **AI-assisted study planning:** Generate a suggested weekly plan using deadlines, estimated task effort, priorities, and the student's available hours; allow manual edits before saving.
3. **Adaptive rescheduling:** When tasks are delayed or skipped, propose a revised plan without silently changing confirmed deadlines.
4. **Study-session tracking:** Mark sessions complete, record time spent, and compare planned versus actual effort to improve future estimates.
5. **Study material organization:** Attach links and notes to each course or task so students can quickly find relevant resources.
6. **Optional shared milestones:** Share selected group-project tasks and progress with invited collaborators; private notes stay private by default.

## Differentiators
- **Academic context first:** Connects courses, exams, assignments, and study sessions instead of treating every task as a generic to-do item.
- **Explainable AI suggestions:** Shows why a task is prioritized (for example, deadline proximity and estimated effort) and lets users override recommendations.
- **Realistic workload planning:** Respects user-defined study hours and avoids scheduling overlapping sessions.
- **Privacy by design:** Keeps personal notes and schedules private by default and shares only information the user explicitly selects.

## Initial Scope (MVP)
The first version will support account registration, course and task management, deadline tracking, availability entry, AI-generated weekly study plans, and manual plan editing. Study-session tracking and collaboration can follow in later releases. The product will be a responsive web application.

## Success Criteria
- Students can create a course, add an assignment, and generate an editable weekly study plan.
- Every planned session is linked to a course or task and fits within the student's stated availability.
- Users can update a task's status and request a revised plan without losing existing task data.
- In pilot feedback, students report whether the generated plans are understandable and practical enough to follow.
